Jellyfish 7 is out!

-   This release is around 60 ELO stronger than Jellyfish 6 in self
    play.
-   SMP algorithm has been changed from YBW to Lazy SMP.
-   Gary Linscott is now officially listed as a co-author.

Download it from [https://Jellyfishchess.org/download/](https://Jellyfishchess.org/download/).
